As we [reported in the summer](https://rism.info/new_at_rism/2026/07/01/Editorial-Network.html), after the closure of the RISM Editorial Center (Zentralredaktion) a newly-founded [RISM Editorial Network](https://rism.info/editorial-network.html) took over the main editorial tasks related to the RISM database. However, while the five members of this Network – the Academy of Sciences and Literature in Mainz, the Bavarian State Library in Munich, the Saxon State and University Library Dresden, the Berlin State Library, and the Johannes Gutenberg University of Mainz – successfully managed the transition to a new editorial structure, some details of both our official cooperation and the practical workflows needed a final polish, prompting the first in-person meeting of the entire team. Thus, on 19 and 20 November, in a lunch-to-lunch format, altogether 15 colleagues gathered in the Berlin State Library to review our experiences to date and seek further to optimize our joint work.
 
The fact that the RISM Editorial Network brings together institutions from four different cities inevitably proves a challenge especially to colleagues who have taken on new editorial tasks and thus need intensive feedback and advice from longer-standing RISM colleagues who happen to be sitting in offices hundreds of kilometers away from them. We are particularly grateful to these new colleagues for all their care and dedication, and hope that our in-person meetings last week could not only bring answers to several concrete questions but also paved the way for more effective personal communication in the future. At any rate, the meeting certainly helped us all to think of us as a single team in which every member brings a vital contribution, whether supervising incoming catalogers, assisting with the development of the Muscat application, curating some specific subset of the vast RISM data pool, or helping us better communicate with the larger RISM community by responding to user queries or curating the central website.

{% include image file="/images/news/2026-11/REN_2026_Gruppenbild.jpg" %}

Besides tackling such current issues, the meeting of the RISM Editorial Network – which we tend internally to simply refer to as REN – also provided a welcome opportunity to think together about some longer-term perspectives for RISM as a whole. Several potential goals have been proposed that go beyond the daily editorial work for which the REN was primarily established, but toward which the five partners should certainly strive, optimally with the help of some third-party funding. In that regard, we may indeed hope that our acronym proves to be prophetic, and the RISM Editorial Network will be able to contribute to a RENovation, even a kind of RENaissance, of RISM as a whole. Needless to say, all of that in close cooperation with the [RISM Digital Center](https://rism.digital) as the other substantial pillar supporting RISM’s core infrastructure, and also the [RISM Consortium](https://rism.info/events/2026/10/01/new-rism-consortium.html) which from next year on also promises to provide a substantial contribution on behalf of the wider international RISM community.
